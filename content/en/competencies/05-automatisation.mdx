title: "Automation thinking"
question: "Could you spot a process ready to be automated, and one that is not?"
summary: "Identify inefficient processes to redesign before, or instead of, automating them."
---
Automating a broken process only makes it fail faster. This capability always starts with the same question: what should be removed or simplified before technology enters the conversation?

You learn to separate digitisation, automation and transformation, three ideas that get conflated with very different consequences.
