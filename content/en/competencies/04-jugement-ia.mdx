question: "Can you tell an AI opportunity from a hype cycle?"
summary: "Assess where AI creates value, its limits, its data requirements, and what has to stay under human supervision."
---
Generative and predictive AI is neither a universal solution nor a gimmick. It creates value in specific contexts, and real risk in others: reliability, confidentiality, bias.

This capability is not about memorising tools. It is about building a decision framework: when to use AI, when not to, and where human verification stays non negotiable.
