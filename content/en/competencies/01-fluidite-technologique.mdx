title: "Technology fluency"
question: "Do you understand what your technical team is telling you?"
summary: "Understand the parts of a modern digital environment, applications, cloud, APIs, data, without becoming an engineer."
---
A capable leader does not configure servers. But they understand what an architecture implies for their organisation: what an integration actually changes, what moving to the cloud means in cost and risk, why an API is not a technical detail.

This capability changes technical meetings. Instead of absorbing jargon, you ask the question that moves the decision.
