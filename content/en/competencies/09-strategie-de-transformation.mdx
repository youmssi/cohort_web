question: "Could you prioritise three initiatives when you can only fund one?"
summary: "Connect digital initiatives to organisational objectives, and know what to prioritise or decline."
---
A strategy is not a list of fashionable initiatives. It is a sequence of priorities justified by expected value, risk and dependencies.

This capability marks the shift from a leader who follows a trend to a leader who arbitrates between options with explicit reasoning.
