title: "Digital risk"
question: "Would you know how to act in the first hour of a security incident?"
summary: "Recognise and manage cyber, operational and vendor risk, without becoming a security specialist."
---
A leader does not need to run a firewall. They need to know what is critical, what can go wrong, and which decisions are theirs when an incident happens.

Identity and access, data protection, business continuity, vendor dependency: this capability builds the governance reflex for digital risk.
