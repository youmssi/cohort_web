title: "Systems thinking"
question: "Do you see the system before proposing a solution?"
summary: "Understand dependencies, processes and organisational complexity before acting on any single point."
---
An organisation is a system: customer, process, people, data, technology, control, outcome. Automating one link without looking at the others usually moves the problem rather than solving it.

This capability builds the reflex of mapping before intervening, and of anticipating how a change lands on the parts of the system nobody was watching.
