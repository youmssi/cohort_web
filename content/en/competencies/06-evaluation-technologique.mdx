title: "Technology evaluation"
question: "Could you assess two vendors without simply picking the cheaper one?"
summary: "Compare solutions on business fit, total cost, risk and dependency, not on the sticker price alone."
---
Money is rarely lost because the technology was bad. It is lost because the buying decision was badly structured. Build or buy, SaaS or bespoke, total cost of ownership, data portability: these are governance questions, not technical preferences.

This capability gives you a repeatable framework for any technology investment decision.
