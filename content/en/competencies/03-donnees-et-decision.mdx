title: "Data and decisions"
question: "Can you turn information into a reliable decision?"
summary: "Tell data, evidence and truth apart, and know which evidence would genuinely improve a decision."
---
A number is not a conclusion. Before acting on data, a capable leader interrogates its source, its method, its period and what it does not show.

This capability structures how you turn information into a recommendation: observation, evidence, interpretation, uncertainty, decision.
