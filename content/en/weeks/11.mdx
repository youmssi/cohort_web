Technology does not equal adoption. Adoption does not equal transformation. A perfectly delivered system can sit unused when the processes, people and governance around it have not changed.

This week introduces the mechanics of organisational change: readiness, communication, incentives, support. These are the conditions that decide whether a technology actually changes how work happens.
