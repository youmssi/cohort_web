A dashboard reports that customer complaints rose by 20 percent. A capable leader neither celebrates nor panics. They interrogate the measurement: over what period, which segment, calculated how, and what is not represented in the number at all.

This week closes the first phase. You learn to structure an Executive Data Brief: observation, evidence, interpretation, uncertainty, recommendation. It is the format you will reuse for every technology decision in the programme.

It is also the first collective checkpoint. The group challenges your interpretation, not your conclusion.
