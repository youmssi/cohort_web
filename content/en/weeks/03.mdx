A company automates one administrative task and, three months later, discovers that another department's workload has grown. The work did not disappear. It moved.

This week builds the reflex of mapping before acting: customer, process, people, data, technology, control, outcome. You pick a real process, often customer onboarding or an internal approval, and trace its current state.

The aim is not to fix it yet. It is to learn to see the system before proposing a solution.
