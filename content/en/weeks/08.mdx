Organisations rarely lose money because the technology was bad. They lose money because the technology decision was badly made.

This week closes the second phase. You build a decision matrix covering business fit, total cost of ownership, security, integration and vendor dependency, then defend your recommendation to peers who are explicitly allowed to attack it.
