This week is not a tour of tools. You design an AI assisted workflow for a real activity, such as report preparation, synthesis or research, then identify precisely where human responsibility must remain non negotiable.

The objective is a reflex you keep long after the programme ends: AI does not replace judgment, it changes where judgment has to be applied.
