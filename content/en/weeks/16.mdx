Twenty minutes of presentation, followed by questions designed not to trap you but to test how solid your reasoning is. Why this problem, why now, which alternatives did you rule out and on what grounds, and what happens if your central assumption turns out to be wrong.

This is not an academic exam. It is the demonstration that you can carry an argued technology recommendation into a real organisation, and defend it.
